# Bronze Age roundhouse reference

Modelled from photographs of two UK reconstructions:

- **Butser Ancient Farm, Dunch Hill roundhouse** (1300–1000 BCE), photo by
  Tim Sheerman-Chase, CC BY 4.0 —
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bronze_Age_Roundhouse_Reconstruction.jpg>
- **Beeston Castle roundhouse** (English Heritage reconstruction), photo by
  Jeff Buck, CC BY-SA 2.0 —
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Beeston_Castle_Bronze_Age_Roundhouse_reconstruction.jpg>

The images are kept only as modelling reference and are not shipped in the
saver. The mesh, texture atlas and shaders in this folder are original work.
